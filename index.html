About Me

Background & focus

I'm a Software Engineer with an MSc in Embedded Systems & IoT from Newcastle University, UK (Merit), and a BTech in Electronics & Computer Engineering from REVA University (CGPA 9.16/10). I work across the stack - from low-level firmware on ESP32, ARM and RTOS platforms, to Python ML pipelines, cloud-connected IoT systems, and data analytics dashboards. I currently teach embedded systems and C programming as an Assistant Professor, and previously built predictive-maintenance and anomaly-detection pipelines for live IoT sensor data as a freelance embedded software engineer.

My interests sit at the intersection of embedded systems, IoT, and applied machine learning - I like taking a problem from raw sensor signal to a deployed, production-ready decision system. I'm the author of 8 filed patents and one IEEE-published paper (ICAECC 2023), and I take a research-driven approach to building reliable software.
